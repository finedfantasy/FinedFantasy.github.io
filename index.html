Privacy Policy

Fined Fantasy · Last updated: October 7, 2026 · Versión en español

Fined Fantasy is a fantasy football game for groups of friends. Fines and the pot are fictional: no real money changes hands and no betting is involved. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who we share it with, and what rights you have, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD), the ePrivacy rules, and U.S. state privacy laws (such as the California CCPA/CPRA).

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: Fined Fantasy (independent developer)
Privacy contact: finedfantasy@gmail.com

3. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b)): account, leagues, notifications and comments.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): security and prevention of fraud and abuse.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): personalized advertising and the use of device identifiers for it. You can withdraw it at any time (section 5).

4. Who we share data with

  • Other managers in your league can see your name, your squad, your activity and your comments.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) as the provider of sign-in, notifications (Firebase), advertising (AdMob) and purchases (Google Play).
  • Infrastructure providers that host the service, if any.

We do not sell your personal data for money. Some transfers to the United States rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

5. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, app-open and interstitial). Premium users do not see ads.

  • Before any ad is shown, the app displays Google's consent form (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) or the U.S. state privacy options notice where applicable. You can accept, decline or manage your choices.
  • If you do not consent to personalization, you will only see non-personalized (contextual) ads, or none if Google cannot serve them without consent.
  • Changing your mind: in the app, go to Settings → Account → "Ad privacy". You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • U.S. residents (California and other states with their own law): advertising based on your activity may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out through the same control or by emailing us. We honor opt-out signals recognized by law (such as Global Privacy Control) when the consent system receives them.
  • How Google uses data from ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account or league exists. If you are removed from a league or the league is deleted, we delete or anonymize its data within a reasonable time, except what we must keep by legal obligation. Technical logs are kept for the minimum time needed for security.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to finedfantasy@gmail.com. We will reply within one month (45 days for requests from California residents, extendable as the law allows).

If you live in California or another U.S. state with a privacy law, you also have the right to know what data we process, to have it deleted or corrected, to opt out of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal data and we do not use sensitive information to infer characteristics about you. You may ask an authorized agent to act on your behalf.

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local supervisory authority.

8. Children

Fined Fantasy is not directed to children under 14 (the age of digital consent in Spain), and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (COPPA). If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures: encrypted connections when the service is published over HTTPS, hashed credentials, restricted access to data, and backups. No system is infallible; if a breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will notify you in the app. The date above shows the latest version.
